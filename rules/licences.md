# Licences: what others may do with our work

**Status (20 Nov 2026):** the owner's decisions of 20 November 2026, by question card. phobos-modkit's and phobos-rimworld's
`LICENSE` files carry them from 1.60.0; every other repository takes them up when it is next worked in.

Read before writing or changing a repository's `LICENSE`, starting a new repository, or preparing a mod's first public page.

## The rules

- **Code, data and documents are MIT** (the standing practice in every repository, confirmed by the owner on 20 Nov 2026): source,
  Defs and other game data, scripts, tests and documents. Frameworks are made to be built on, and a restrictive licence would only
  keep other modders away from them.
- **Our art and sound are CC BY 4.0** (owner, 20 Nov 2026): textures, icons, pictures and sounds we make, AI-assisted ones included.
  Anyone may reuse and change them, in any mod, with credit to House D'thorga. Not covered: a recording in the public domain (such
  as DVIDS footage) keeps that status and its own credit, and nothing of a game's or another author's is ours to license.
- **Our names are not part of either grant** (owner, 20 Nov 2026): "D'thorga", "House D'thorga" and "Phobos D'thorga", the names
  of our mods and tools, and their logos and Workshop preview images. A fork or reupload uses its own name and preview and says
  what it is based on. Why: reuploads of mods are common on the Steam Workshop, and players blame the original author for a fork's
  faults when the fork carries the original's name. The code stays fully open; only the names are kept, as Rust and Firefox do.

## The text a LICENSE carries

Each repository's `LICENSE` keeps its MIT text and its own scope paragraph (what the repository holds, what it excludes, a game's
terms such as Ludeon's EULA), and adds these two paragraphs after the scope:

```text
Art and sound: the textures, icons, pictures and sounds made by Phobos A. D'thorga (House D'thorga) for this repository, including
those made with AI image and sound tools, are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit: "House D'thorga (dthorga)". This does not cover recordings in the public
domain, which keep that status and their own credit, or anything excluded above.

Names: neither licence grants the use of the names "D'thorga", "House D'thorga" or "Phobos D'thorga", the names of this
repository's mods and tools, or their logos and Workshop preview images. A fork or reupload must use its own name and preview, and
should say what it is based on.
```

A repository with no art or sound keeps the paragraph all the same, so the rule reads the same everywhere.

## Limits

- **Copyright in AI-made pictures and sounds is unsettled**, in Australia and elsewhere. Where no copyright exists there is nothing
  to license, so the art paragraph states what we intend more than what a court would enforce.
- **A licence already granted cannot be taken back**: what a repository published under MIT stays usable under MIT. These rules
  only add clarity; they remove no right anyone had.
- The names are kept by the licence's own words, not by a registered trademark.
