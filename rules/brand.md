# The brand: names and public pages

Read before naming anything players or the public see, and before writing a public page (a README's opening, a Workshop
description). Moved here from phobos-rimworld and D'thorga Companion on the owner's word (23 Nov 2026: "Move all 12 now"); the
rules are the owner's of 9 Nov 2026.

- **D'thorga is the one brand for everything new, in every game** (owner, 9 Nov 2026).
- **Names people see are plain and unassuming**, so people can look them up and remember them (owner, 9 Nov 2026: "It's all
  about brand awareness"): `D'thorga <Plain Word>`, as in D'thorga Framework and D'thorga Companion, however lovely a fancier
  name is. The word after D'thorga says what the thing does. **Check a name is not already taken** in that game's mods before
  using it.
- **Older Phobos-named mods take the new display name when the owner next returns to that game**, never in a sweep. Display
  names only: package IDs, internal identifiers, repository names and anything a save can hold stay as they are.
- **Every public page also carries the spelling "dthorga"**, without the apostrophe, in the Workshop description and the README's
  opening, so a search spelled either way finds us (searching GitHub or the web for "dthorga" once found none of our work). A
  repository may check it, as phobos-rimworld's mod layout check does.
